## Pinned source facts; do not substitute conjecture

Pristine `PurpleI2P/i2pd@635b013a612ff47278ef02acf8580a28e10e26c5`:
- `libi2pd/NetDb.cpp::LoadRouterInfo` reads signed files and inserts eligible floodfills into `m_Floodfills` **during startup**; `NetDb::Start` then tests `m_RouterInfos.size() < reseed.threshold || m_Floodfills.GetSize() < NETDB_MIN_FLOODFILLS`, and `libi2pd/NetDb.hpp` defines **`NETDB_MIN_FLOODFILLS = 5`**. Therefore `reseed.threshold=0` alone does not produce a normal, adequately seeded floodfill environment if just one RI is present. Do not call an attempted reseed proof of peer-selection failure or route to the public internet to satisfy it.
- `RouterInfo::IsEligibleFloodfill` requires >= `NETDB_MIN_FLOODFILL_VERSION` **0.9.62**, a published IPv4 address (or documented eligible IPv6 combination), and a **non-DSA** signing identity. `NetDb::GetClosestFloodfill` selects admitted, non-unreachable, not-profile-unreachable, non-excluded routers; it cannot choose an entry missing from that set.
- `reservedrange` is enabled normally and rejects loopback on input; the test-owned option `reservedrange=false` must be explicitly restricted to this isolated private fixture. RouterInfo `netId`, `router.version`, signature, freshness, exact published NTCP2 `s`/`i`/host/port, and active floodfill role are prerequisites. No `R`, `O`, `P`, false version or public reachability fabrication is necessary to satisfy the pinned i2pd eligibility function.
- Pinned `NetDb::Load` prints **`floodfils`** for loaded floodfill count; Plan 463's diagnostic filtering for `floodfill` can omit that source-owned startup line. Fix the **test-owned** parser, not the reference code.

**Capability:** an honest stock candidate eligible for normal source-owned NetDB operations (not yet proof of a connection). **Infrastructure:** startup signed-RI corpus and categorical admitted/not-admitted receipt. **Invariant:** exact-pinned unmodified stock processes, no external reseed, no false source capability, no RouterInfo mutation or identity grinding. **Polish:** clear preflight failures.
