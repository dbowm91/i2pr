## First failure and source preconditions

- `tests/integration/core-router-recovery/run_plan458_sam_product.py` starts four pinned pristine i2pd **2.61.0** (`PurpleI2P/i2pd@635b013a612ff47278ef02acf8580a28e10e26c5`) peers, each `notransit=false`, `floodfill=true`, `bandwidth=L`, and each with a distinct signed family. It cycles starts and RouterInfos so stock peers have current family-aware signed RI before i2pr selects tunnel participants.
- `crates/i2pr-daemon/src/service_product.rs::provision_all_service_router_material` currently selects **three remote hops** per path via validated RouterInfo candidates and sends short builds through the real SSU2 owner. A very small four-peer fixture risks duplicate-hop, family, profile, insufficient selectable candidates, or stock overload. **These are hypotheses, not an established root cause.**
- In pinned i2pd, `libi2pd/TransitTunnel.cpp` accepts/rejects short/ECIES build requests according to `AcceptsTunnels`, limited-connectivity status, congestion, available/requested transit bandwidth, duplicate next hop and tunnel registration. The build may reject before or after authenticated arrival. `libi2pd/Tunnel.cpp::HandleShortTunnelBuildMsg` also handles a creator's reply vs forwarding an incoming transit request.
- Plan 465 recorded **4 emitted, 4 authenticated replies, 4 installed** followed by an incomplete destination summary. Plan 466 recorded **3 emitted, 2 authenticated replies, 2 installed** with the third unmatched before deadline; peer acceptance/reply emission **not established**. The first cause is between source-owned stock acceptance/forwarding and i2pr reply delivery, not automatically an i2pr codec error.

**Capability:** real `i2pr run` owns nonzero-hop inbound/outbound SAM destination tunnels, valid signed LS2, and bidirectional external `STREAM CONNECT/ACCEPT` byte-exact payload. **Infrastructure:** source-backed stock acceptance and concise stage receipt; explicit, safe small-hop **diagnostic** where compatible with normal configuration. **Invariant:** no synthetic/second router, fabricated BuildReply, tunnel-zero-hop shortcut, daemon identity leak, raw peer paths/keys, public role or modified stock implementation. **Polish:** reasoned readiness and concise failure stage.
