## Problem and architectural decision

crates/i2pr-daemon/src/service_product.rs::provision_all_service_router_material initiates inbound service-destination builds through ssu2_handle.delivery() and waits for inbound installation before beginning queued outbound builds. crates/i2pr-daemon/src/exploratory_build.rs::submit_path sends a ShortTunnelBuild directly to the first selected peer through the router transport. Controlled direct SSU2 construction may qualify lower-layer interoperability, but **normal** inbound destination tunnels should use an established outbound tunnel as their delivery path, with gateway-directed garlic protection where specified, rather than directly revealing the creator-to-inbound-gateway relationship. Pinned i2pd Tunnel.cpp selects outboundTunnel->SendTunnelDataMsgTo(...) with router-targeted wrapping for this case, while its no-outbound branch is the bootstrap exception. The current direct-first product cannot be treated as a full privacy-preserving router just because signed LS2 and owners eventually install.

The earlier 0/2 inbound owner discrepancy led to a correct Plan 474 transactional owner/generation fix; deterministic 2/2 tests now pass. A subsequent exact-pin SAM replay failed earlier at build return. Preserve the generation fix. Do not replace strict SAM readiness or synthesize lease/owner state.

## Capability / infrastructure / invariant / polish

**Capability:** ordinary daemon creates independent nonzero-hop exploratory and destination tunnels, sends inbound build requests over qualified outbound paths after bootstrap, publishes genuine signed LS2, and transfers exact bidirectional remote SAM STREAM payload under churn. **Infrastructure:** bootstrap-to-exploratory transition, one shared transport-neutral RouterDeliveryService (SSU2 and optional NTCP2), coherent tunnel manager lifecycle, typed source-owned build and application readiness. **Invariant:** router/destination identity unlinkability, no normal direct gateway dial except tightly scoped bootstrap or explicit controlled evidence profile, no fake zero-hop application tunnels, no second daemon/socket/NetDB owner, bounded cancellation, no cross-destination receive-ID or generation confusion. **Polish:** redacted readiness reason taxonomy and no fixture-specific product code.
